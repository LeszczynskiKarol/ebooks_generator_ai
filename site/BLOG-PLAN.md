# InkMagnet — blog content plan

Built from the product's verified advantages and the 2025/2026 competitor pricing research.
Each post gets an EN version (`/blog/<slug>`) and a PL version (`/pl/blog/<slug>`) unless marked PL-only.
Priority: **P1 = high commercial intent (write first)**, P2 = supporting/education.

Legend — "Leans on": the advantage/comparison the post is built around. "Links to": internal target.

---

## A. Comparison & "vs" posts (highest commercial intent — people searching with a wallet open)

1. **P1 — "Best AI ebook generators in 2026 (honest comparison)"**
   Angle: round-up of Designrr, Sqribble, Squibler, Youbooks, Atticus/Vellum vs InkMagnet. Segment the market: *draft-text generators* vs *typesetting-only tools* vs *finished-book generators*. InkMagnet is the only one that researches + writes + typesets + covers in one one-time payment.
   Keyword: "best AI ebook generator", "AI book generator comparison". Links to: /pricing, /vs/*.

2. **P1 — "InkMagnet vs Designrr: generating a book vs repurposing your content"**
   Angle: Designrr = $29–249/mo subscription that reformats *existing* content; InkMagnet generates an original, researched book one-time. (Refresh the existing /vs/designrr page with current pricing.)
   Keyword: "designrr alternative", "designrr vs". Links to: /vs/designrr.

3. **P1 — "InkMagnet vs Sqribble: template filler vs a real written book"**
   Angle: Sqribble = $67 one-time but YOU fill templates with YOUR text; InkMagnet writes and typesets it for you.
   Keyword: "sqribble alternative". Links to: /vs/sqribble.

4. **P1 — "Atticus & Vellum are $147–250 — and they don't write a single word"**
   Angle: typesetting tools only format a manuscript you already wrote. InkMagnet writes it AND typesets it for less. Strong price-anchor piece.
   Keyword: "atticus alternative", "vellum alternative", "cheaper than vellum".

5. **P1 — "Can't I just use ChatGPT or Claude to write an ebook?"**
   Angle: expand the landing's comparison — chat gives you a truncating wall of text from memory; InkMagnet researches the live web, keeps a 120-page book consistent, and hands you a typeset PDF + EPUB.
   Keyword: "chatgpt ebook", "write a book with chatgpt". Leans on: research grounding + whole-book consistency.

6. **P2 — "Subscription vs one-time: why we charge per book, not per month"**
   Angle: most AI book tools are $30–90/mo; InkMagnet is one-time per book with full commercial rights. Make the "no subscription" a feature.
   Keyword: "AI ebook generator no subscription". Leans on: pricing model.

   Keyword: "cost to create an ebook", "ebook creation cost".

8. **P2 — "What you actually pay a ghostwriter (and what you get instead)"**
   Angle: realistic ghostwriting/formatting market rates; position InkMagnet as the 95%-cheaper finished alternative for non-fiction/lead magnets.
   Keyword: "ghostwriter cost", "ebook ghostwriter price".

9. **P2 — "Free AI book generators: what's the catch?"**
   Angle: free/credit tools give unformatted drafts, watermarks, or no commercial rights. What "finished and sellable" really requires.
   Keyword: "free AI ebook generator".
